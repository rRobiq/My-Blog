Home Page

Welcome to my personal blog—I'm Tomescu Robert, and I’m thrilled to have you here.

Music isn’t just a hobby for me—it’s a lifestyle. From the moment I wake up to the time I wind down at night, music flows through every part of my day. It inspires me, energizes me, and helps me express emotions words sometimes can’t capture.

I'm especially drawn to hip-hop, a genre that has profoundly shaped my identity and perspective. Artists like Young Thug and Playboi Carti aren’t just on my playlists—they’re voices that resonate with how I see the world. Their music is raw, experimental, and incredibly dynamic. Whether it's Thug’s unorthodox melodies or Carti’s infectious energy and flow, there's something in their art that always keeps me coming back for more.

The beauty of music is that it adapts to every moment. Whether I’m studying, walking through the city, or just zoning out with my headphones on, there’s always a beat that fits the vibe perfectly. Hip-hop gives me the freedom to feel confident, creative, and connected to something bigger.

This blog is a space where I’ll share more about my musical tastes, artists I’m currently listening to, and how music influences different aspects of my life. So, if you’re a fellow music lover or just curious about my world, you’re in the right place.

Stay tuned—this is just the beginning.
