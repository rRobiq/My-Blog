How can a music streaming platform can see my favorite artist was my question for today and i learned that so let me explain that for you
So a music streaming platform determines your top artist by analyzing your listening habits. It tracks data like:

Streaming platform methods

  • Play Count: How often you play songs by an artist.
  • Listening Time: Total minutes spent on their music.
  • Skips & Replays: If you frequently replay or skip their songs.
  • Interaction: Likes, shares, or playlist additions.

Based on this data, the platform compiles a list of your most-listened-to artists. It’s a cool way to see who you’ve been vibing with the most.
